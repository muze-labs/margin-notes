Margin Notes Example

This is a simple example of the margin-notes component. Try adding notes to the marked paragraphs.

Paragraph 1: Dependencies are not a problem in themselves. They become a problem when they are invisible, when they are not managed, and when you cannot see or control what depends on what.

Paragraph 2: A well-designed system makes its boundaries explicit. The interfaces between components are declared, the data flowing across them is typed, and the contract is enforceable.

Paragraph 3: Linked data preserves facts that the application does not understand. This matters because tomorrow's application might understand them, and the user's data should survive the transition.
